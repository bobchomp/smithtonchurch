Why we give

Thank you for giving to the ministry of Smithton Church. We consider each gift an important expression of worship. Whether you give online, or during a Sunday service, we appreciate the generous way you support God’s work and we are committed to being faithful stewards of God’s resources.

With online giving, you can give to Smithton Church with recurring or one-time gifts. Online contributions are accepted through bank account, debit card, or credit card.

Give online

You can make an online payment directly to the church. This also allows Gift Aid to be included where applicable, and supports the General Fund of the church.

Make a donation

Ways to give

Bank transfer

Electronic fund transfer from your bank account. This requires your bank name, account type, account number and sort code.

Credit card

You will need to provide your card number, expiry date, security code, billing address and card holder’s full name.

Cheque

Make cheques payable to Smithton Church and post to: Smithton Church, Murray Road, Smithton, Inverness, IV2 7YU.

Phone or email

Telephone the church office on {{ site.church.phone }} or email {{ site.church.treasurer_email }}.
