A message from our Missions Committee

At Smithton Church, we are privileged to support various individuals, couples, and organisations involved in mission work both locally and globally. Our committee meets regularly to distribute funds, update the congregation, and foster prayerful interest in our mission partners.

For more information on how you can show your support, please view the full list of our partners’ prayer points below or speak to any committee member.

Blessings,
The Missions Committee
