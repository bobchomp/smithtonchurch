A church at the heart of the community

As a church at the heart of the community, we welcome community use of our conferencing facilities. Smithton Church is a modern building with a variety of rooms also available for hire by external organisations.

There are additional meeting rooms of various sizes with free wifi throughout the building. We offer a complete solution for your event, using the very latest in digital technologies, modern catering facilities, and a large car park with spaces for around 200 vehicles.

Costs on application from the church office. Call {{ site.church.phone }} or send us a message.
